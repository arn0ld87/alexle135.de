# Page Goals & Strategy

## 1. Homepage (`/`)

- **Goal:** Immediate understanding of "Alexander Schneider - IT-Systemintegration & DevOps".
- **Target Audience:** Recruiters, Potential Clients, Peers.
- **CTA:** "View Projects" / "Contact Me".
- **KPIs:** Bounce Rate, Click-through to Projects/Contact.
- **Key Content:** Hero Section, Skills Overview, Featured Projects, Contact Snippet.

## 2. About Me (`/ueber-mich`)

- **Goal:** Establish trust and personality. Show the human behind the code.
- **Target Audience:** Recruiters, Team Leads.
- **CTA:** "Download CV" / "Contact".
- **KPIs:** Time on Page, CV Downloads.
- **Key Content:** Bio, Career Timeline, Personal Values, Soft Skills.

## 3. Services (`/leistungen`)

- **Goal:** Clear definition of what is offered.
- **Target Audience:** Clients, Business Partners.
- **CTA:** "Request Quote" / "Book Discovery Call".
- **KPIs:** Conversion to Contact Form.
- **Key Content:** distinct service packages, process explanation, pricing (if applicable).

## 4. Projects (`/projekte/*`)

- **Goal:** Proof of competence. Show, don't just tell.
- **Target Audience:** Recruiters, Clients.
- **CTA:** "View Live" / "View Code".
- **KPIs:** Click-through to external links.
- **Key Content:** Case studies, tech stack used, challenges solved, results.

## 5. Contact (`/kontakt`)

- **Goal:** Conversion.
- **Target Audience:** Everyone.
- **CTA:** Submit Form.
- **KPIs:** Form Submissions.
- **Key Content:** Simple form, social links, availability status.

## 6. Blog (`/blog/*`)

- **Goal:** Authority and SEO traffic.
- **Target Audience:** Developers, Tech Enthusiasts.
- **CTA:** "Subscribe" / "Read Related".
- **KPIs:** Organic Traffic, Shares.
- **Key Content:** Technical tutorials, insights, industry news.

## 7. AI Agent Lab (`/ki-agenten-lab`)

- **Goal:** Showcase cutting-edge capability in AI.
- **Target Audience:** Innovation Labs, Tech Forward Clients.
- **CTA:** "Try Demo".
- **KPIs:** Interaction time with agents.
- **Key Content:** Demos, explanations, interactive agents.
